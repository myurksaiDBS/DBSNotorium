## 1. Getting Started

### Create your account

1. Visit the sign-in screen and click **Create an account**.
2. Enter your **name**, **email**, and a **password** (minimum 8 characters).
3. You'll be logged in automatically and your **14-day free trial** begins.

### Sign in

Enter the email and password you used at signup. Sessions are kept in a secure HttpOnly cookie, so closing the tab won't sign you out. Click **Sign Out** in the sidebar when you're done.

> **Locked out?** After **5** failed attempts on the same email you're locked out for **15 minutes**. Wait it out or contact your firm admin.

### The 14-day trial

- During the trial you have **full access** — create entries, upload seals, send certificates, everything.
- The Dashboard and every screen shows a banner with **days remaining**.
- On day 15, writing new records is blocked until you subscribe (reading and exporting still work).

## 3. Journal Entries

The Journal is where every notarial act is recorded.

### Create a new entry

1. Go to **Journal → New Entry**.
2. Fill the form:
   - **Date & time** (auto-filled to now).
   - **Notarization type** — Acknowledgment, Jurat, Oath / Affirmation, Copy Certification, Signature Witnessing, Protest.
   - **Document type & description**.
   - **Signer info** — name, address, phone, email.
   - **ID info** — type, number, issuing state/country, expiration.
   - **Optional witness** name.
   - **Fee** (defaults to 0 if free).
   - **Notes** and the **digital signature** (draw on the pad).
3. Click **Save**. The entry is stored, and the signer is auto-added to your Clients list if new.

### View, edit or delete an entry

Each row has three icons on the right:

- **Eye** — read-only view, sends a share-link option and shows email history.
- **Pencil** — edit any field, including re-signing.
- **Mail** — email the certificate PDF to the signer.
- **Trash** — delete with confirmation.

### Filter entries

Above the table:

- **Search** — matches signer name, document, ID number.
- **Type filter** — Acknowledgment / Jurat / etc.
- **Start / End date** pickers.
- **Clear** wipes all filters.

Filters update the list live.

## 6. Intake Links — Get Signer Info Before You Meet

Intake Links let signers fill out their own details from their phone before your appointment. **They never see your journal — only what you asked for.**

### Create an intake link

1. **Intake Links → New Intake Link**.
2. **Label** (private to you): e.g. "Smith closing – Thu 3pm".
3. **Appointment note** (shown to the signer): e.g. "Bring your driver's license".
4. **Signer email** — required if you want the 24-hour automatic reminder.
5. **Appointment date & time** — required if you want the 24-hour automatic reminder.
6. Choose how long the link stays valid (24h → 30 days).
7. Click **Create Link**.

### Share the link

The Share dialog opens automatically. From it you can:

- **Copy Link** — paste anywhere.
- **Copy Text** — pre-written SMS you can paste.
- **Open in Messages** — launches your phone/computer messages app pre-filled.
- **Download QR Poster (PDF)** — Domini-branded flyer with the link + QR code. Great for walk-in signers.

### The signer's experience

Signers land on a branded page showing **your firm logo, name, and website** (or Domini's if you haven't uploaded a firm logo yet). They fill in name, address, phone, email, ID details, and can add notes. When they tap **Send to my Notary**, the info is locked to your account.

### Automatic 24-hour reminders

If you set **signer email** *and* **appointment date & time**, we automatically email the signer roughly **24 hours** before the appointment if they haven't submitted yet. You'll see a "Sent reminder" badge on the row after we send it.

Prefer to nudge them earlier? Click the **Remind** button on the row — an on-brand reminder email goes out immediately.

### Use a completed intake

When the signer has submitted, the row status turns green (**Ready**). Click **Use →** to pre-fill a new Journal entry with everything they submitted — you just add the ID number verification, fee, and signature at the appointment.

## 7. Commission Details & Renewal Reminders

Head to **Commission** in the sidebar (or Settings → Commission).

- **Notary name**, **Commission number**, **State**, **Commission expiration date**.
- Toggle **Commission Alerts** on / off.
- When alerts are on, we email you at **60, 30, 14, 7, and 1 days out**, on **expiration day**, and again if it lapses. You never get the same bucket twice, so no email fatigue.
- Click **Send preview to my inbox** to see exactly what a real alert looks like.

## 8. Firm Branding

*Settings → Firm Branding.* This section is **critical for multi-notary firms** because it appears on:

- The public signer intake page.
- The PDF certificate footer.
- Email invitations to colleagues.

Fields:

- **Firm name** — e.g. "Yurksaitis Notary Journal".
- **Firm address / phone / website / license number** — optional but printed on your certificates and journal PDFs.
- **Firm logo** — PNG or JPEG, ≤ 500 KB. Displayed beside the Domini shield on intake portals and above certificate signatures.

Click **Save Firm Profile** when done. Certificates you email from now on carry the new footer.

## 9. Notary Seal

*Settings → Personal Brand.* Upload a PNG or JPEG (≤ 500 KB) of your notary seal. When set, it prints beside your name at the bottom of every certificate PDF.

- **Upload** — pick a file (transparent background looks best).
- **Remove** — go back to the plain typed-name signature.

## 11. Certificates & Email Delivery

Every completed entry can generate a **certificate PDF** — the Domini watermark shield, your seal, firm footer, and full act detail.

### Download

Open an entry (eye icon) → **Download Certificate**. The PDF saves locally.

### Email the certificate to the signer

Click the **mail icon** on the entry row:

1. Prefills the signer's email from the entry.
2. Add an optional personal message.
3. Click **Send**.

The signer receives the PDF attached to a Domini-branded email. Every send is logged in **Email History** (viewable inside the entry) and appears on your Dashboard delivery card.

### Bounce digests

If any certificate email bounces or fails, we email you a **morning digest** the next day listing what failed with one-click **Open & Resend** links. This runs daily at 8:30 UTC — you can also preview it any time from *Settings → Delivery*.

## 12. Search Presets & PDF Export

### Save a filter set

Set up filters on the Journal (search + type + start + end date). Click **Save current filter as preset**, give it a name (e.g. "Q1 refi closings"), and it's stored.

- Preset chips appear above the table.
- Click one to **apply**.
- Click **X** on a chip to delete it.
- Each preset has an **Export PDF** button that produces a full journal PDF for that exact filter — perfect for auditors.

### One-off PDF export

Even without a preset, click **Export PDF** in the header to download whatever your current filter is showing.

Every exported journal PDF has:

- The Domini watermark shield on every page.
- Your firm logo (if uploaded) at the top of page 1.
- A red top rule and the firm name in the footer.
- Continuous page numbering.

## 13. Billing & Subscription

*Billing* in the sidebar.

### Choose a plan (after your trial)

- **Notarium Pro — Monthly**: $9 / month.
- **Notarium Pro — Yearly**: $90 / year (Best Value badge).

Click **Start … — 14 days free**. You go to Stripe Checkout — enter a card (test card `4242 4242 4242 4242`, any future expiry & CVC in demo). On success we activate your subscription automatically and return you to the app.

### Manage your subscription

Once subscribed, **Manage Billing** launches the **Stripe Billing Portal** where you can:

- Update your card.
- View invoices.
- Change plan.
- Cancel (access stays until the current period ends).
- Resume (if you cancelled).

### Status badges

- **Trial** — inside the 14-day window.
- **Active** — subscription live.
- **Past Due** — payment failed; card update needed to avoid lapse.
- **Canceled** — cancelled at the end of the paid period; still functional until then.
- **Expired** — trial or subscription ended; writes are blocked.

## 15. Security & Account

*Settings → Security.*

- **Change Password** — enter current password, then new one twice. Minimum 8 characters.
- Sessions live in a secure HttpOnly cookie — you're immune to typical XSS token theft.
- Bcrypt hashing on the server side.
- Failed-login lockout as described in *Getting Started*.

If you suspect account compromise, change your password immediately — all sessions stay valid until they naturally expire, so also **Sign Out** everywhere by closing tabs.

## 16. Troubleshooting & FAQs

**Q. My PDF certificate looks blurry.**
Make sure your uploaded seal is a **high-resolution PNG on a transparent background**. JPEGs on white also work but the transparency looks cleaner.

**Q. The signer says the intake link says "expired".**
The link's expiration is set when you create it (default 7 days). Create a new one — you can use the exact same appointment note.

**Q. I entered an appointment time in my local time zone. Did the reminder work?**
Yes. The app converts your `datetime-local` input to UTC before storing, so the 24-hour window respects the actual time you set — no matter which zone you're in.

**Q. I don't want commission reminders.**
Settings → Commission Alerts → toggle off. You can also disable the delivery-bounce digest by not opening it — but we recommend leaving both on.

**Q. Can I hide the Domini watermark on my certificates?**
The Domini watermark is part of the platform's brand of trust and cannot be removed, but your firm logo appears alongside it (and above your name), so the certificate is unmistakably yours.

**Q. What happens to my data if I cancel?**
Nothing disappears. Your entries, signers, seal, and firm branding stay intact. You'll still be able to sign in and export PDFs; you just can't create new records until you subscribe again.

**Q. The billing page is stuck loading after checkout.**
We poll Stripe for up to 20 seconds after redirect. If it never updates, refresh — the webhook usually completes within a few seconds.

**Q. How do I download my journal for an audit?**
Journal → set your date range → **Export PDF**. Or save a preset for that range and use its **Export PDF** button on the chip.

**Q. Where do I get help?**
Email your firm's super-admin (visible in Roster), or reply to any Notarium system email — replies route back to Domini Business Services support.
